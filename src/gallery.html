About Us

KelingKeling was built for the wanderers who prefer the side streets to the main roads. We’re a small collective dedicated to uncovering the quiet corners and vibrant pulses of our coast. No generic tours, no tourist traps—just the honest, local rhythm of the places we call home.Our mission is to bridge the gap between being a visitor and belonging, stripping away the "must-see" lists to focus on the textures and hidden corners that make a destination truly unique.
